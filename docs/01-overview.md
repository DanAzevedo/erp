This project grew out of my appreciation for management systems that deliver value to businesses across industries, including manufacturing, retail, and services. The goal is to develop an affordable ERP system, primarily for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) that lack the resources to invest in more comprehensive solutions.

The system aims to provide automation, performance metrics, and operational controls that simplify business management and support informed decision-making.

The project also serves as a practical learning experience and a professional portfolio piece, helping me strengthen my skills and demonstrate them to recruiters. In the future, I intend to develop it into a SaaS product.

At this initial stage, development focuses on the foundations of user and company management, including administrators, user-company relationships, and access control.
